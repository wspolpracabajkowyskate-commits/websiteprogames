**EN — full (~80 words)**
PRO GAMES (PRO GAMES POLAND Sp. z o.o.) designs and manufactures commercial boxing arcade machines in Jastrzębie-Zdrój, Poland. The range covers Boxer Standard machines, premium boxers, boxing-and-kicking combos such as Double Hit and Monster 3 in 1, kids models and strength games. PRO GAMES supplies arcade operators, venues and distributors and provides spare parts and service directly. The brand has been active since 2012; UPlay America is its exclusive distributor for the Americas. Official website: https://www.progamespoland.com/
