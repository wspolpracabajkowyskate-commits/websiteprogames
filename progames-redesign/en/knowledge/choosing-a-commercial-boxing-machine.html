How to choose a commercial boxing machine

Choose the game format and installation requirements first, then the finish and optional equipment. A useful quote should identify the exact model and the configuration being supplied.

1. Define the venue and the players

For a bar, family entertainment centre or amusement park, describe the expected player group, supervision and intended location to the supplier. The venue label alone does not establish that a model is suitable. Outdoor placement requires explicit confirmation for the selected machine.

2. Compare space and access

Use the product’s published dimensions and weight as a starting point. Check the delivery route, doorways and floor location. Ask for the required operating clearance and electrical documentation; a cabinet footprint does not include the space needed for play and maintenance.

3. Select the game format

Compare a dedicated boxing machine with a boxer-and-kicker combination, a multiplayer boxer or a kids model. The right choice depends on the attraction you want to offer, rather than an unsupported forecast of earnings.

Standard collection · Boxer Fist · Double Hit Kids

4. Confirm payments and customisation

The configurator lets you request equipment such as an additional coin slot, bank-note acceptor, custom stickers and Internet Wi-Fi Boxnet. Compatibility and availability require sales confirmation. A Wi-Fi option does not by itself establish cashless-payment support.

If you need card, cashless or venue-system payments, name the system and market in your enquiry. Ask about compatibility, installation responsibility and any third-party charges before ordering.

5. Check the full quotation

Confirm the machine version, included equipment, transport, lead time, warranty terms and service contacts. Keep the specification with your order so the supplied configuration can be checked.
