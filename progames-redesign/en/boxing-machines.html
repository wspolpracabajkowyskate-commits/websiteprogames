Commercial boxing machines for sale: what to confirm before ordering

To request a quotation from PRO-GAMES, choose a model and tell sales where it will be installed and delivered. Use the product configurator to describe the colour and equipment you want; the team confirms the final specification, compatibility and commercial terms.

Which boxing machine format should I compare?

Start with the Boxer Standard collection for a punching game, Double Hit for boxing and kicking, or Monster 3 in 1 for boxing, kicking and hammer play. Compare the published information for the exact model rather than assuming that every cabinet has the same features.

What information should I send for a commercial quotation?

  • The model, preferred colour and number of machines.
  • The venue type and delivery country.
  • Available floor space and access for delivery.
  • Requested payment equipment, graphics and other options.

Can I choose payment equipment and custom graphics?

The configurator records your requested equipment and custom stickers. Sales must confirm availability and compatibility for the model and destination. A Wi-Fi option alone does not establish support for a card-payment system.

Where can an operator find spare parts?

Use the spare-parts catalogue and contact service@progames.pl with the model and details of the part. Product quotations go to office@progames.pl.

Can the machine be installed outdoors?

Ask sales to confirm the permitted installation conditions for the exact version before ordering. Do not assume that all boxing arcade machines have the same outdoor protection.
