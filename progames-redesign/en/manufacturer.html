A European manufacturer with a product-led range

PRO-GAMES is a Polish manufacturer of commercial boxing arcade machines, based at ul. Rybnicka 19A, 44-335 Jastrzębie-Zdrój, Poland. The brand has operated since 2012; the current company is PRO-GAMES POLAND Sp. z o.o.

The range covers Boxer Standard designs, premium boxing machines, combined boxing and kicking games, kids models and strength testers. Selected additional attractions are distributed products and are identified as such.

Questions about PRO-GAMES

Is PRO-GAMES a manufacturer or a reseller?

PRO-GAMES is the manufacturer of its boxing machines, boxing-and-kicking combos, kids boxers and strength games. A small group of additional attractions (air hockey, basketball, Kiddie Ride, Cyberdart, inflatables) is distributed and marked as such on every product page.

What is the official PRO-GAMES website?

The official website is https://www.progamespoland.com/. The Polish version is at /pl/, and progames.pl — also used for company e-mail — redirects there. Some older directory listings link to boxer-progames.com; that address no longer works.

Are Pro Games Poland, PRO-GAMES POLAND and PRO-GAMES the same company?

Yes. Industry directories and partners have used these spellings for the same Jastrzębie-Zdrój manufacturer. The brand is PRO-GAMES; the legal entity is PRO-GAMES POLAND Sp. z o.o.

Can I buy a boxing machine directly from the manufacturer?

Yes. Send the model, quantity, destination and required equipment to office@progames.pl or use the quote form. Buyers in North, Central and South America are served by UPlay America, the exclusive distributor for the Americas.

Can the machine be customised?

Each product page has a configurator for colours and equipment such as an additional coin acceptor, a banknote acceptor, custom-graphic stickers and Boxnet Wi-Fi. Sales confirms which options fit the exact model and destination.

Where do operators get spare parts and service?

Directly from PRO-GAMES service: service@progames.pl, +48 789 108 086. Part names are listed in the spare-parts catalogue.
