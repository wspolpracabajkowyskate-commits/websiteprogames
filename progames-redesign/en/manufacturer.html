ProGames: boxing machine manufacturer in Poland

ProGames develops and manufactures commercial boxing arcade machines and amusement equipment in Poland. The company works with operators and distributors and provides sales, spare-parts and service contacts.

A European manufacturer with a product-led range

The ProGames brand has operated since 2012. The current company, PRO GAMES POLAND Sp. z o.o., is based at ul. Rybnicka 19A, 44-335 Jastrzębie-Zdrój, Poland. Brand history is distinct from the registration date of the current legal entity.

The range covers Boxer Standard designs, premium boxing machines, combined boxing and kicking games, kids models and strength testers. Selected additional attractions are distributed products and are identified as such.
