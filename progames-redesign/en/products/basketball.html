DISTRIBUTED / BASKETBALL

Basketball

Our weatherproof basketball arcade machines can be used outdoor all year round. The game has four levels of difficulty and comes with a set of five balls. Depending on the mode selected, the basket can be movable.

It is very easy to assemble and transport.

Basketball is a game for all ages with both kids and adults loving this game. Players can play in a group and compete for the best score (linking option). It's a high quality long lasting machine. WIK is a leader in making the basketball arcade machines with years of experience. This is a crowd pleaser that never ceases to provide fun.
