Boxing machine supply for operators and distributors

For a single venue, a multi-machine order or a distribution enquiry, contact PRO-GAMES with the intended market and a product shortlist. Commercial terms are confirmed individually.

What to include in a wholesale enquiry

  1. Company name, contact details and delivery country.
  2. Machine models and requested quantities.
  3. Preferred colours, artwork and requested payment equipment.
  4. Venue type or intended distribution territory.
  5. Target delivery date and any access constraints.

Ask sales to confirm pricing, minimum order quantities if applicable, lead time, transport, warranty terms and parts support in writing. No standard discount, minimum quantity or delivery time is promised on this page.

Existing distribution and local support

The website lists UPlay America as the exclusive distributor for North, Central and South America. For that territory, start with the official partner or ask PRO-GAMES to route your enquiry.

Interested in becoming a distributor?

Introduce your company, territory, existing sales and service capabilities, and the machines you would like to represent. PRO-GAMES will confirm whether a partnership is available and which arrangements apply. An enquiry does not establish territorial rights.
