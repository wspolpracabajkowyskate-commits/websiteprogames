How to play

Three moves, straight from the MONSTER 3IN1 machine: boxer, kicker and hammer.

PUNCH

J or X

Breaks crates and knocks out enemies in front of you. Crates hide tokens and hearts.

KICK

K or C

Kicks a bouncing ball that flattens enemies in its path. Great against electric Sparkies.

SMASH

↓ in the air

Hammer drop: crushes cracked floors, stuns everything around and launches you off springs. On the ground ↓ breaks cracked walls.

Tips

  • Jump on Baggers and Glovers – but never on electric Sparkies.
  • Fill the RAGE bar to unleash MONSTER RAGE: 7 seconds of invincibility.
  • Every 100 tokens gives an extra life. Arcade machines along the way save your progress.
  • BIG BAG can only be hit while it lies dazed after a slam. Jump over its shockwaves!

MONSTER 3IN1 – boxer, kicker and hammer in one cabinet
