WHO WE ARE

POLISH ENGINEERING.
REAL ENTERTAINMENT.

Pro Games Poland specialises in boxer machines and commercial amusement equipment. The company develops and builds machines in Poland, while supporting operators with sales, spare parts and service.

The Pro Games brand has operated since 2012 and supplies customers in Europe and beyond.
