## Geometry comparison

Preserved: broad canopy and base, approximately half-width central column, front-facing hanging bag, separate lower ball in a recessed opening, overhanging upper shell, dark trim, rear service layout, primary factory artwork and lighting locations. Base front was revised from individual rectangular posts to one rounded perimeter frame after visual comparison. Canopy title was moved onto the curved front face, and the separate score screen aligned with the source graphic. Custom-branding UV orientation was corrected after rendering an uploaded text logo.

Approximations: exact depth, all real dimensions, fine seams/screws, upholstery wrinkles, printed gradients, football stitching, underside mechanism, precise LED sockets and additional hardware. The lower silhouette and canopy curvature follow the reference but are not a manufacturing reconstruction. Optional hardware is absent from supplied views and intentionally labelled schematic in the UI and documentation.

Scale: roughly 2.17 m high × 1.26 m wide × 0.88 m deep in model coordinates, chosen for reference proportions. Do not use for transport planning, fabrication or print die-cutting. Existing website dimensions for Double Strike 2 were not assumed to certify the generated reference machine. Manufacturer dimensions and decal templates are required for production approval.

Deliverable is a functional first-model prototype. It is not described as a photorealistic scan or a measured CAD asset.
